How it works

Plan a trip or a power cut before it happens. Tell us how long you'll use each device and see if one charge covers it.

  1. Pick a moment, or start from zero. The buttons fill in a typical night out, shoot or power cut. You can change anything after.
  2. Set the hours. Use + and − to say how long each device runs. A fan on for 8 hours, lights for 4.
  3. Read the result. The big percentage is how much of one full charge you'd use. Under 100% means one charge is enough. Over 100% means add solar or an extra battery.
