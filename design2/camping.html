Weekends that don't end at sunset.

Singapore families pitch tents at East Coast, Pasir Ris and Pulau Ubin to swap screens for sea breeze. The hard part comes at 10 pm, when it's 28 °C in the tent and the phones are dying.

Shop nowPlan your night

You're not alone out there

NParks issued nearly 70,000 camping permits in 2023, about 50% more than in 2019. Most went to families with young children, and more than half were for East Coast Park.

Source: The New Paper, September 2024.

What park camping in Singapore asks of you

  • A free permit for each tent, booked online with Singpass or at an AXS station. Apply early: at least two weeks ahead is the current guidance.
  • No open fires, gas stoves or LPG at park campsites. Plan on ready-to-eat food, and on electric power for light.
  • Designated areas only: East Coast Park (Areas D and G), Pasir Ris Park (Areas 1 and 3), West Coast Park (Area 3), plus Pulau Ubin's Jelutong, Mamam and Endut Senin sites.

Rules change. Check the NParks and SLA pages before you book.

Going for two nights on Ubin?

Bring a foldable solar panel. In strong sun, River 3 Plus takes up to 220W and can refill over a long lunch. Or add an extra battery for 572Wh.
