Where it earns its keep

  • Golden-hour shoots

    Marina Barrage, Botanic Gardens, Sentosa. Keep batteries cycling and a light running until the last frame.

  • Tethered and on-site editing

    Run a laptop off the 100W USB-C port and send previews before the client leaves.

  • Pop-up stalls and events

    Lights, a fan and a card terminal at weekend markets, all quiet enough to talk over.

Commercial shoots at some parks and attractions need a permit, so check venue rules first. River 3 Plus is 286Wh, above the 160Wh airline limit, so it stays home when you fly.
