Camping

Your first night at East Coast Park

Most first-time campers worry about the wrong thing. Setting up the tent takes twenty minutes. What actually ends the night early is heat, mosquitoes and a phone at 4% when the kids want one more photo.

Book the permit first

Camping permits for East Coast, Pasir Ris and West Coast Parks are free, but you need one for each tent. Book online with Singpass or at an AXS station, and do it early. Weekends in the school holidays go fast. Each tent takes up to six people, and the campsite runs from 9 am to 9 am the next day.

Pick your spot for the breeze

Areas D and G at East Coast face the sea. Arrive before 5 pm to set up while there's light, and pitch the door facing the water to catch the evening wind.

Plan for a warm night

Open fires and gas stoves aren't allowed at park campsites. Bring dinner ready to eat, and plan your power for the two things that matter after dark: light and air. A small USB fan running all night is the single biggest upgrade to a camping trip in Singapore.

What we'd pack

  • Tent with mesh panels, plus a groundsheet
  • Mosquito repellent, and long, light sleeves for the evening
  • A USB fan and warm LED string lights
  • River 3 Plus, charged to 100% the night before
  • Water, more than you think, plus a trash bag to leave nothing behind

Golden hour, flat batteries

Every location photographer knows the moment. The light is finally perfect, the couple is finally relaxed, and the camera flashes a red battery icon. The last spare went flat an hour ago.

Know your numbers

A typical mirrorless camera battery holds around 16Wh. River 3 Plus holds 286Wh, so even after conversion losses it can refill a camera battery more than a dozen times, or keep two chargers running all afternoon while you shoot.

Charge in rotation, not at the end

Set up the charger as soon as you arrive. Swap a battery in every time you change setups, and you'll finish the day with more full batteries than you started with.

Light and laptop from the same box

A 40W LED light for fill and a laptop on the 100W USB-C port can run together, well inside the 600W limit. Edit the first previews on site, and the client leaves with a smile instead of a promise.

Check the venue

Some parks and attractions need a permit for commercial shoots. Sort that out first, then plan your power.

Care

Looking after a LiFePO4 battery in the tropics

Lithium iron phosphate cells are tough. River 3 Plus is rated for 3,000 cycles to 80% capacity. A few habits help you get all of them.

Keep it out of a parked car

River charges between 0 and 45 °C and works best at 20–30 °C. On a sunny afternoon, the inside of a parked car in Singapore can get much hotter than that. Bring it with you, or leave it in the shade.

Give it air

Don't cover the vents with towels or sleeping bags, especially when it's running a fan or charging.

Don't forget it in the cupboard

If it's sitting unused, top it up every few months so it doesn't drain too low. If it's set up as home backup, it stays plugged in and you never have to think about it.
