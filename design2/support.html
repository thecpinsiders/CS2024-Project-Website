Support and FAQ

Straight answers to the questions people ask before, and after, their first trip.

Before you buy

Will it run a rice cooker or kettle?

Often, yes. River 3 Plus has 600W of continuous output. X-Boost lets heating appliances up to 1200W run at reduced power, so a travel kettle up to 1200W works but boils more slowly. Kettles above 1200W won't run. Heating uses a lot of energy, so one battery won't last through many litres.

Can it run an air-conditioner?

Not in a useful way. Even small portable air-conditioners draw close to the 600W limit, so they'd drain the battery within minutes. For sleeping in the heat, a fan is the better choice.

Is 286Wh enough for me?

For one night of lights, a fan and phones, yes, with room to spare. For two nights or bigger devices, add solar charging or an extra battery. Our runtime calculator shows your own setup.

Charging

How long does it take to charge?

About 60 minutes from 0 to 100% on a wall socket. Solar charging at up to 220W can take as little as 1.5 hours in strong sun, and the car's 12V socket takes about 3.3 hours.

Can I use it while it's charging?

Yes. That's how home backup works: River stays plugged in, powers your devices and switches to battery if the mains drops.

Travel and safety

Can I take it on a plane?

No. Airlines generally don't allow lithium batteries above 160Wh, and River 3 Plus is 286Wh. It's built for road trips and campsites.

Is it safe to use inside a tent or flat?

Yes. It doesn't burn fuel, so there are no fumes. Keep the vents uncovered, keep it dry and keep it out of direct sun.

Can I bring it into Malaysia by car?

It's a battery, not fuel, so people commonly bring power stations on road trips. Check current customs rules before you travel.

Warranty and orders

What warranty do I get?

Five years when you buy from an authorised seller. Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase.
