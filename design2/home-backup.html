When the lights go out, nothing else has to.

Singapore's grid is one of the most reliable in the world. That's why, when the power does trip at 9:40 pm, nobody's ready for it.

Shop now

It's rarely a blackout. It's usually one of these.

  • A tripped circuit at night

    The kettle and the air-con overload the circuit, and the flat goes dark while you hunt for the DB box.

  • A scheduled power shutdown

    Upgrading or maintenance works sometimes need a planned cut. You get a notice, but your Wi-Fi doesn't read it.

  • A storm, or a trip up north

    Family homes in Johor or a holiday kampung don't always have Singapore's grid.

Set it up once, then forget it's there

Plug River 3 Plus into the wall, and plug your router, desktop or fish-tank pump into River. If the power drops, it switches to battery in under 10 ms. The EcoFlow app shows how much charge is left.

Running a medical device such as a CPAP? Check with the device maker about power requirements before relying on any backup.
