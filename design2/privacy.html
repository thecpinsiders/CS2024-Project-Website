Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026. How Bluehour Studio handles personal data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

1. What we collect

When you request the Stay Longer Kit, we collect your name, email address, optional mobile number, the moment you selected and any question you write. We don't collect NRIC numbers, payment details or anything we don't need.

2. Why we collect it

  • To send you the kit and reply to your question.
  • If you separately ticked the marketing box: to send deals and news by email, SMS or WhatsApp.

We won't use your data for any other purpose without asking you first.

3. Consent and withdrawal

We only collect data after you tick the consent box. Marketing consent is separate and optional. You can withdraw either at any time by emailing dpo@bluehour.studio or using the unsubscribe link in any email. We'll act on it within 10 working days and tell you what withdrawal means, for example that we can no longer reply to your enquiry.

4. Do Not Call Registry

We only send marketing messages to Singapore numbers when you've given clear consent in the form, or after checking the number against the Do Not Call Registry.

5. Who we share it with

Only the email service that delivers our messages, under a written agreement to protect your data. We never sell or rent personal data. If data is stored outside Singapore, we make sure it's protected to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

6. How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after your last contact with us, then we delete it securely, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. How we protect it

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with access limited to named team members, protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication. If a breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we'll notify you and the PDPC as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you and how we've used it in the past year, and ask us to correct anything that's wrong. Email our Data Protection Officer and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies, analytics and third-party content

This site doesn't set advertising cookies. We'd like to use analytics to count visits, but only if you choose Allow in the banner. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can reset it by clearing site data. Fonts load from Google Fonts and photos from Unsplash, which receive your IP address to serve them.

10. Contact our Data Protection Officer

Data Protection Officer, Bluehour Studio
Email: dpo@bluehour.studio

This is a student project. The form on this site doesn't send data to any server unless the team connects a form service (see the README).
