Safety mark

Look for the ST-SIRIM label

In Malaysia, electrical products that need approval from the Energy Commission (Suruhanjaya Tenaga, or ST) carry an ST-SIRIM label. It's a hologram sticker showing the product has a Certificate of Approval and was tested through SIRIM.

EcoFlow Malaysia says its power stations are certified by SIRIM QAS and Suruhanjaya Tenaga.

Source: EcoFlow Malaysia (ecoflow.com.my), checked November 2026. That statement covers EcoFlow's power stations in general, and we haven't seen a certificate that names River 3 Plus, so we'd rather you check the label than take our word for it.

Three checks that take two minutes

  • Find the ST-SIRIM label on the unit or the box when it arrives.
  • If the listing doesn't show certification, ask the seller for the Certificate of Approval (COA) number before you pay.
  • Buy units sold through EcoFlow's authorised Malaysian channel. Grey imports can carry another country's certification and may not get the local warranty.

Before you check out

A few minutes now saves a headache later.

  • Check that the seller is EcoFlow or an authorised Malaysian reseller, so your local warranty is valid. EcoFlow Malaysia lists 2 years on River 3 Plus, and some authorised resellers offer up to 5. Check what your listing says.

  • Confirm the model: River 3 Plus is 286Wh. River 3 is a smaller, different model.
  • Look for bundles with an extra battery or a solar panel if you plan multi-night trips.
