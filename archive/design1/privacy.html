Privacy policy · Effective 29 October 2026

Your data, handled with care.

This policy explains how Kindling Studio handles personal data collected on this website, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). The short version: we collect only what we need, use it only for what we told you, and delete it when we're done.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Kindling Studio ("we", "us"), a student agency running the "Keep Home Close" campaign for the RIVER 3 Plus marketplace seller. It is a university project and is not operated by EcoFlow.

Data Protection Officer: dpo@kindlingstudio.example

2. What we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to give us through the "Get your setup" form:

  • Your name and email address (required).
  • Your mobile number (optional).
  • Your answers about what you want to power, your budget and how you heard about us (optional).
  • A record of which consent boxes you ticked and when.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and recommend a suitable setup.
  • To send you a website-only voucher related to your enquiry.
  • To send tips and promotions by email, only if you ticked the optional marketing box.
  • To contact you by SMS or WhatsApp, only if you gave a mobile number and ticked that box.
  • To understand which channels bring visitors, using the optional "where did you hear about us" answer, reported in aggregate.

We will not use your data for any other purpose without first asking you.

4. Consent and withdrawal

By ticking the required box on the form, you consent to the collection and use described in section 3. Marketing consent is separate, optional and unticked by default. You can withdraw any consent at any time by emailing our Data Protection Officer or using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. We will stop the relevant use within 10 working days. Withdrawing consent may mean we can't complete an enquiry that is still open.

5. Marketing messages and the Do Not Call Registry

We only send marketing messages to people who have opted in. Before sending marketing messages to a Singapore phone number, we check it against the Do Not Call Registry unless you have given us clear and specific consent to contact that number.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with:

  • The service providers that host this website and deliver form submissions to us, under their own security and privacy terms.
  • The RIVER 3 Plus seller we work for, only if you ask us to pass on an order or warranty question.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Some providers may store data outside Singapore. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data receives protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your form submission for 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete it. If you withdraw consent or ask us to delete your data, we do so sooner unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. How we protect it

Only team members who need your data can access it. We use secure (HTTPS) connections, password-protected accounts with two-factor authentication, and we never store form data in public files or code repositories.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used in the past year, to correct anything that is wrong, or to delete it. Email dpo@kindlingstudio.example and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to cause you significant harm, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Commission as required by the PDPA.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version, and we'll tell people on our email list about significant changes.
