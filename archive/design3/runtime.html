Where to buy

How long
will it run?

Tap the devices you’d plug in. Each one gets a line on the label with an estimate of how long it would run on its own, and the total shows how long they last together.

What this tells you

Before you buy, check that RIVER 3 Plus can run your things, and for how long. We’ve started the label with a typical home desk.

  1. Tap what you’d plug in

    Choose from the list, or type the watts of anything else. Watts are printed on the device’s charger or label.

  2. Read each row

    Each row shows how long a full charge runs that one device by itself. A 10 W Wi-Fi router runs about 24 hours.

  3. Check “All together”

    The big number is how long everything lasts when it all runs at the same time. If it says “Over 600 W”, run fewer devices at once.

How we work it out

RIVER 3 Plus stores 286 Wh1. Converting that to AC power loses some energy as heat, so we assume 85% is usable: 286 × 0.85 ÷ watts = hours. Real results depend on the device, the temperature and how hard it’s working.

Where to find a device’s watts

  • Look on the charger or the label underneath. It shows watts (W), or volts × amps.
  • Laptops rarely draw their charger’s full rating, so a 65 W charger usually averages 30–45 W.
  • Fridges and cooler boxes cycle on and off, so use the average figure, not the peak.

The limits

  • 600 W in total across the AC outlets. Anything above that trips the protection.
  • Heating appliances up to 1,200 W, like a kettle, can use X-Boost1. It lowers the voltage, so they heat more slowly.
