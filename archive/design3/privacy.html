Privacy
policy.

Last updated 1 November 2026. This explains what personal data this site collects, why, and your rights under Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA).

Who we are

This site is a student project by Datum Studio (Group [No.]) for a university web design module. We’re not EcoFlow, and we don’t sell products. Purchases happen on Lazada or Shopee under their own privacy policies.

What we collect

Only what you type into the setup guide form: your name, email, country, your main use and, if you add one, a message. We don’t collect payment details, and we don’t ask for any national ID number.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the setup guide you asked for. This needs your consent through the required checkbox.
  • To email you about deals on the official store, only if you tick the optional box.

Consent and withdrawal

Both checkboxes start unticked. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us. We’ll stop using your data for that purpose and tell you what that means, for example that you won’t get deal alerts.

Access and correction

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, how we’ve used it, and to have it corrected. We’ll respond within 30 days.

How long we keep it

Only as long as we need it for the purpose you agreed to, and no later than the end of the project. Then we delete it.

Protection

Access is limited to our team members, on password-protected accounts. We never sell or rent your data, or share it with EcoFlow or the marketplaces.

Cookies and third parties

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads. Fonts and images are stored with the site, so no third party receives your IP address when a page loads. Links to Lazada, Shopee and our sources take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

Demo notice

In this student version, the form checks your answers in your browser and shows a confirmation. Nothing is sent to a server or stored.

Contact and Data Protection Officer
