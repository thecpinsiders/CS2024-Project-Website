Outdoors · Family camping and road trips

The campsite, with the comforts switched on.

Most families don't skip camping because of the outdoors. They skip it because of the heat, the dead phones and the 2 am meltdown. Take those away and the trip happens.

One night at Pasir Ris

From pitch to sunrise, on one charge.

A family of four, one tent, one RIVER 3 Plus. Here is how the evening actually uses the battery.

  1. 4:30 pm

    Pitch, then plug in

    Tent up, RIVER 3 Plus in the shade of the vestibule. At 4.7 kg, the nine-year-old insists on carrying it from the car.

  2. 7:15 pm

    Lights on, phones full

    The lantern runs from one USB port. Two phones top up on the others while dinner comes off the booked barbecue pit nearby.

  3. 8:30 pm

    Movie on the tent wall

    A mini projector plays one film for about 60 Wh. Ask the kids later and this is the part they remember.

  4. 10:00 pm

    The fan that saves the night

    It's still 28°C. A USB camping fan runs on low until morning for around 105 Wh, quietly enough that all you hear is the sea.

  5. 8:00 am

    Coffee, courtesy of the sun

    A 220W solar panel has been out since sunrise. With some charge back, X-Boost runs a small kettle for one boil, well away from the tent.

The energy budget

233 Wh used. Room to spare.

About 243 Wh of the 286Wh battery is usable after conversion losses. This night uses 233 Wh, and the morning sun refills the rest.

Camping in Singapore, the calm way

Five things worth knowing before you go.

Collected from campers, park rules and a few lessons learned the sweaty way.

  • Permit
    Book a free NParks camping permit first

    Overnight camping is only allowed at designated areas, such as Areas 1 and 3 at Pasir Ris Park. You apply online in advance with a Singapore address and ID. School holidays fill up fast.

  • Cooking
    No stoves or grills near the tent

    Park rules don't allow camping stoves or portable grills near tents. Book a barbecue pit for dinner, and keep any kettle use well away from your tent.

  • Heat
    Keep the power station in the shade

    It performs best between 20°C and 30°C. Tuck it in the vestibule or under a chair, never in direct afternoon sun.

  • Rain
    Off the ground, under the fly

    It isn't rated as waterproof, so a sudden downpour is a real risk. Keep it raised and covered.

  • Further
    Road trips across the Causeway

    Driving to Desaru or Mersing? Charge it from the car socket on the way (about 3.3 hours for a full charge) and arrive with a full battery.
