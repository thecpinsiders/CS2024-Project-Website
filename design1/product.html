Highlights

Small box. Very few compromises.

All of it serves one idea: you shouldn't have to think about power while you're busy living.

  1. Runs what matters

    600W across three AC outlets covers fans, laptops, projectors, routers and CPAP machines. X-Boost lets heating appliances up to 1200W, like a small kettle, run on their own.

    600W rated · 1200W X-Boost · 1200W surge

  2. Full in about an hour

    Plug it into the wall before dinner and it's ready before you leave. On the road, charge from the car socket or a portable solar panel.

    AC 0 to 100% in about 60 min · solar from 1.5 h at 220W

  3. Quiet enough to sleep beside

    GaN power electronics waste less energy as heat, so the cooling fan rarely needs to work hard.

    Under 30 dB at light loads

  4. Catches you when the power drops

    Use it as a UPS between the wall and your devices. The EcoFlow app shows how much charge is left during a long outage.

    Switchover under 10 ms

  5. Built for years of use

    LiFePO4 cells and X-Guard battery management. Light enough to carry in one hand.

    3,000 cycles to 80% · 4.7 kg

  6. Grows when your plans do

    Connect one Extra Battery to reach 572Wh or 858Wh. Check charge and output from your phone in the EcoFlow app, over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

    Expandable to 858Wh · app control
