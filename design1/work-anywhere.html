Your office is wherever the view is.

Remote work promised freedom, then sent you hunting for the one café seat near a socket. RIVER 3 Plus moves the socket to wherever the light is good.

A workday with no wall

Standup to sunset, on one charge.

A freelance designer and videographer, one shoot, one edit, two calls. This is how the day spends the battery.

  1. 8:30 am

    Standup from the beach

    The laptop sits on the 100W USB-C port and charges at full speed during the call. Under 30 dB, the mic picks up the waves and nothing else.

  2. 11:00 am

    A location with no outlets

    Three camera batteries take turns on the charger while you shoot. None of them run out before the light changes.

  3. 2:00 pm

    Edit in the shade

    A portable monitor runs for four hours of review with the client, and your phone stays topped up for messages.

  4. 6:30 pm

    Pack up, plug in, done

    Back at home base, 60 minutes on the wall and it's full for tomorrow.

Made for this kind of work

What matters when your desk keeps moving.

  • USB-C
    100W, straight to the laptop

    Charge most laptops at full speed without the AC adapter brick, which also wastes less energy.

  • Sound
    Quiet on calls and recordings

    Under 30 dB at light loads, so it won't ruin a voice-over or a client call.

  • Recharge
    Full again in an hour

    Plug in over lunch at a co-working space and you're set for the afternoon shoot.

  • Home office
    A UPS for your desktop

    At home, keep it between the wall and your PC. It switches over in under 10 ms, so the computer stays on.

  • Travel
    Road trips, not flights

    At 286Wh it is above airline limits, so it stays on the ground. Perfect for drives to shoots in Johor or a staycation workweek.
