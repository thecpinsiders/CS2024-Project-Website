FAQ and support

Good questions, straight answers.

The things people ask before they buy, and the things they wish they'd asked. Can't find yours? Send it to us.

Choosing

How long will it run my fan, CPAP or fridge?

Divide about 243 (the usable watt-hours) by the device's watts. A 15 W camping fan runs around 16 hours, a 45 W standing fan around 5 hours, and a CPAP at 30 W with the humidifier off around 8 hours. For your exact mix, use the Power Planner.

Is 286Wh enough, or should I get a bigger one?

For one night of camping, a remote workday or an evening outage, yes. If you camp for two nights without sun, or need CPAP all night with margin, add an Extra Battery 300 (572Wh total). The good news: you can add it later without replacing anything.

What can't it run?

Anything that draws more than 600W continuously, such as an air-conditioner, a microwave or an induction cooker. Simple heating appliances up to 1200W, like a small kettle, can run in X-Boost mode.

What does X-Boost actually do?

It lets resistive heating appliances up to 1200W, like a small kettle or hair dryer, run by lowering the voltage they receive. They run more slowly than on the wall. It isn't for motors or electronics, and UPS backup is off while X-Boost is active.

Safety and use

Is it safe to use in a tent or a bedroom overnight?

It uses LiFePO4 cells, the stable and heat-tolerant lithium chemistry, managed by X-Guard with more than 40 protections, inside a fire-retardant shell. There are no fumes. Keep it ventilated, dry and out of direct sun, and don't cover it with bedding or bags.

Can I charge it while it powers my devices?

Yes. That's how UPS mode works: plugged into the wall, it passes power through to your devices and switches to battery in under 10 ms if the mains drops.

What if it rains at the campsite?

The battery pack is IP54 rated against dust and splashes, which handles a light shower. It isn't waterproof. Keep it under the fly sheet and off wet ground.

How should I store it between trips?

Somewhere cool and dry, ideally 20 to 30°C. Check the charge in the app every few months and top it up so it's ready when you need it.

Charging and travel

How fast does it charge?

About 60 minutes from a wall socket, as little as 1.5 hours from 220W of solar in strong sun, and about 3.3 hours from a car socket.

Can I bring it on a plane?

No. Airlines limit lithium batteries to 160Wh, and RIVER 3 Plus is 286Wh. It's made for road trips, campsites and home.

Can I take it on a road trip to Malaysia?

Yes. Charge it from your car's 12V socket on the way, and use the car outlet on the station to run a 12V cooler box at the other end.
