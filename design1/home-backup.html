How backup works

Set it up once. Forget it's there.

UPS stands for uninterruptible power supply. It means the battery is already in the loop, ready the instant the wall stops delivering.

  1. Plug RIVER 3 Plus into the wall

    It charges and stays full. The battery isn't being used, so it isn't wearing out.

  2. Plug your essentials into it

    Router, desktop PC, CPAP machine, a lamp. Mains power passes straight through to them.

  3. The power drops

    In under 10 milliseconds it switches to battery. That's faster than most computers and routers can notice.

  4. You decide what happens next

    Check the charge left in the EcoFlow app. Connected by the USB-B cable, a PC can save your work and shut down by itself.

Worth saying plainly

If someone depends on a medical device.

Check the device's power needs with its maker or your doctor first. Many CPAP machines use far more power with the humidifier or heated tube on, so turn those off for backup use, or size up to RIVER 3 Max.

RIVER 3 Plus is a backup power station, not a medical-grade power supply. Keep a second plan, such as a spare battery from the device maker, and know who to call.
