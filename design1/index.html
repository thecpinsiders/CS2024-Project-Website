Home backup

When the lights go out, nothing important does.

Leave it between the wall and your router, PC or CPAP machine. If the power drops, it takes over in under 10 milliseconds. Most devices never notice.

How backup works →
11:40 pm · Pasir Ris Park, Area 1

The kids stopped asking to go home around nine. Now the only sounds are the sea and a small fan turning slowly in the tent. The box beside it has been running for three hours. Nobody has noticed it once.

That's the job. Quiet enough to forget, steady enough to trust.

<30dB

Gallium nitride (GaN) power electronics waste less energy as heat, so the cooling fan rarely has to work. At light loads RIVER 3 Plus stays under 30 dB, around the level of a whisper.

Why it lasts

Bought once. Trusted for years.

It sits beside your children in a tent and beside your router at home. It should be the calmest, safest thing in the room.

  • Battery
    LiFePO4 cells, 3,000 cycles to 80%

    Lithium iron phosphate is the stable, heat-tolerant chemistry. Charge it every single day and it still holds 80% after about eight years.

  • Protection
    X-Guard battery management

    X-Guard battery management watches the LiFePO4 cells, the stable, heat-tolerant lithium chemistry.

  • Backup
    Switches over in under 10 ms

    Keep it plugged in between the wall and your devices. If the mains power drops, they keep running.
