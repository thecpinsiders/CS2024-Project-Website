    How we calculate

    The maths, in one line.

    Hours ≈ 286 Wh × 0.85 ÷ device watts

    Example: a 45 W standing fan gives 286 × 0.85 ÷ 45 ≈ 5.4 hours. We use 85% because converting battery power to 230V AC loses some energy as heat. USB devices are usually a little more efficient, so treat these numbers as a safe floor.
