Add it within 30 days of buying River 3 Plus. Pay once, or yearly.

How renting works

  1. Pick a kit and your dates, then register interest. We confirm availability by WhatsApp.
  2. Collect it fully charged in the Klang Valley, or have it delivered.
  3. Return it within your dates. Buy your own within 30 days and we deduct the full rental fee from the price.

What a kit can run: lights, fans, speakers, laptops, phones and card terminals. What it can't: air-conditioners, deep fryers or a rice cooker all evening. Check your devices.

For pasar malam stalls, food trucks and event crews

Swap the genset for silence. No petrol smell next to the food, no engine noise over your customers, and nothing to refuel between nights.

Monthly stall plans include a swap service: bring back a flat kit, take a charged one, and keep trading.

Cheaper to run than a genset. A full recharge of the 858Wh Stall kit uses about 1 kWh, which costs under RM 1 at typical TNB home rates. A petrol genset burns fuel every night it runs.

Business package: several units on one order, a proper invoice for your accounts, and priority support. For event and wedding crews, film shoots and site offices too.

Size up later

Two kids become three, the weekend trips get longer, the home office grows. Bring your River 3 Plus back within three years and trade it in towards a bigger EcoFlow power station.

  1. Your trade-in value is set the day you buy, so you know it up front.
  2. We check its condition and battery health in the EcoFlow app.
  3. The value comes back as a voucher for your next, bigger station.
