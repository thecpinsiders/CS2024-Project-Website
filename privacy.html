Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026. How Bluehour Studio handles personal data under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA), as amended in 2024. Baca ringkasan dalam Bahasa Malaysia.

1. What we collect

When you request the Stay Longer Kit or register interest in a service, we collect your name, email address, optional mobile number, the moment you selected and any question you write. You give us this data directly. We don't collect MyKad numbers, payment details or anything we don't need.

2. Why we collect it

  • To send you the kit and reply to your question.
  • If you separately ticked the marketing box: to send deals and news by email, SMS or WhatsApp.

Giving us your name and email is needed to send the kit. Everything else is optional. We won't use your data for any other purpose without asking you first.

3. Consent and withdrawal

We only collect data after you tick the consent box. Marketing consent is separate and optional. You can withdraw either at any time by emailing dpo@bluehourstudio.sg or using the unsubscribe link in any email. We'll act on it within 10 working days and tell you what withdrawal means, for example that we can no longer reply to your enquiry.

4. Direct marketing

You can ask us at any time to stop using your data for direct marketing, and we will. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and replying "STOP" to an SMS or WhatsApp message works too.

5. Who we share it with

Only the email service that delivers our messages, under a written agreement to protect your data. We never sell or rent personal data. If data is stored outside Malaysia, we only use places with a law substantially similar to the PDPA, or we make sure it gets an equal level of protection.

6. How long we keep it

Up to 12 months after your last contact with us, then we delete it securely, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. How we protect it

Data is sent over HTTPS and stored with access limited to named team members, protected by strong passwords and two-factor authentication. If there's a data breach, we'll notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours, and tell you without delay if it's likely to cause you significant harm.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct anything that's wrong, and ask us to send your data to another organisation where that's technically possible. Email our Data Protection Officer and we'll respond within 21 days.

9. Cookies, analytics and third-party content

This site doesn't set advertising cookies. We'd like to use analytics to count visits, but only if you choose Allow in the banner. Your choice is saved in your browser, and you can reset it by clearing site data. Fonts and photos are stored with the site, so no third party receives your IP address when a page loads.

10. Contact our Data Protection Officer

Data Protection Officer, Bluehour Studio
Email: dpo@bluehourstudio.sg

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner (Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi).
