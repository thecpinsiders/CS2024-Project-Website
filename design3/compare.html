When it isn’t the one

We’d rather you buy the right thing. Look at something else if any of these sound like you.

  1. Fly

    You need power on a plane

    Anything over 160 Wh is banned from passenger luggage4. Choose a power bank under 100 Wh instead.

  2. >600 W

    You want to run big appliances for hours

    An air conditioner, a microwave or power tools all day need a larger station with a higher rated output.

  3. Phones

    You only charge phones

    A power bank is lighter, cheaper and does that job well.
