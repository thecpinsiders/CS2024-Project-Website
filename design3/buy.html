Before you pay

Five checks, about two minutes, so the box that arrives is the one you wanted.

  1. The seller is the official brand store

    Look for the brand or official store badge next to the seller’s name. It’s the simplest way to make sure the warranty is honoured.

  2. It’s RIVER 3 Plus, 286 Wh

    Not the RIVER 3 (a smaller model) or a Max bundle, unless you want the extra capacity. The title should say 286 Wh.

  3. The plug type matches your country

    Some listings offer more than one regional version. Pick the one with your country’s outlets.

  4. The warranty is written on the listing

    The listing we checked states 5 years2. Screenshot it and keep your order confirmation.

  5. The timing is right

    Big marketplace campaigns (9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12) often bring vouchers. If you’re not in a hurry, get a deal alert.
