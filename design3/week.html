Where to buy

One box,
seven days.

Most power stations get bought for one camping trip and then sit in a cupboard. This one is meant to stay plugged in at your desk all week, and go with you at the weekend.

Monday to Friday: a backup at your desk

Plug RIVER 3 Plus into the wall and your router, laptop charger and monitor into RIVER 3 Plus. Power passes straight through while the mains is on. If it drops, the battery takes over in under 10 ms1.

A typical home office of a router, a laptop and a monitor draws about 80 W, which gives you around 3 hours. That’s plenty to finish a meeting and save your work.

Rate your own desk

What a week looks like

These are example loads with our runtime estimate for each one on its own. The method is explained on the runtime page.

  1. Tue

    A breaker trips mid-call

    The router (10 W) and laptop (45 W) keep going. Together that’s about 4.4 hours.

  2. Wed

    A late night, a quiet room

    Quiet at light loads (under 30 dB1), so it can sit in the bedroom while a desk fan runs for about 9.7 hours.

  3. Thu

    Charge everything before the trip

    USB-C up to 100 W1 charges a laptop at full speed. The two USB-A ports handle phones and earbuds at the same time.

  4. Fri

    Unplug, lift, go

    About 4.7 kg1 with a built-in handle. It takes up roughly the space of a small cooler bag in the boot.

  5. Sat

    Campsite, beach or balcony

    LED lights (15 W) for about 16 hours, or a 120 W projector for about two hours, enough for one film.

  6. Sun

    Top up on the way home

    The car socket fills it in about 3.3 hours, and a 220 W solar panel in as little as 1.5 hours in strong sun1.

Evenings: light when the block goes dark

Power cuts rarely announce themselves. A lamp, phone chargers and the Wi-Fi keep running on one charge, and the EcoFlow app over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth shows how much is left1.

Set up outage backup

Road trips, yes. Flights, no.

At 286 Wh it’s well over the 160 Wh limit airlines allow4, so it can’t go in cabin or checked luggage. It’s made for the car, the campsite and home.
