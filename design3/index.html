Five numbers worth knowing

Every figure links to where it came from. If we couldn’t verify a number, we left it off this site.

  1. 286 Wh

    Stored energy

    That’s nearly four times a 20,000 mAh power bank. Add an Extra Battery for up to 858 Wh3.

  2. 600 W

    Rated AC output

    Enough for laptops, fans, lights and a projector at the same time. Heating appliances up to 1,200 W can run in X-Boost mode1.

  3. 100 W

    USB-C, full speed

    Charges most laptops as fast as their own charger does1, plus two USB-A ports for phones.

  4. 220 W

    Solar input

    Full in as little as 1.5 hours in strong sun1, so a long weekend doesn’t need a wall socket.

  5. <30 dB

    Quiet at light loads

    Under 30 dB at light loads1, so you can keep it in the bedroom or the tent.
