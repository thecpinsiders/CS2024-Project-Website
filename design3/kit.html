kit.

RIVER 3 Plus works on its own. These add-ons are for when your week gets longer or your weekends go further. Buy only what you’ll actually use.

Four ways to go further

Each add-on solves one problem: more capacity, charging off the grid, or charging on the road.

  1. +286 Wh

    Extra Battery 300

    Doubles your capacity to 572 Wh. It connects to the unit and turns it into a RIVER 3 Max.

    Search on Lazada
  2. +572 Wh

    Extra Battery 600

    Takes you to 858 Wh3, enough for longer trips or a longer outage. Sold together as the RIVER 3 Max Plus.

    Search on Lazada
  3. 220 W

    Portable solar panel

    RIVER 3 Plus accepts up to 220 W of solar1. In strong sun a 220 W panel can fill it in about 1.5 hours. A smaller panel works too, just more slowly.

    Search on Lazada
  4. 3.3 h

    Car charging

    Top it up from the car socket while you drive, in about 3.3 hours from empty1. Check that a car charging cable is in the box on your listing.

    Charging questions

Three kits we’d suggest

Desk only: RIVER 3 Plus on its own.

Weekend camper: RIVER 3 Plus with a solar panel, so you never come home flat.

Long outages or family trips: RIVER 3 Max Plus (858 Wh), with solar if you camp more than two nights.
