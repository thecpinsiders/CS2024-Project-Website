Buying

Can I buy RIVER 3 Plus on this site?

No. This site helps you decide. You buy on the official EcoFlow store on Lazada or Shopee, which handle payment, delivery and returns.

Why don’t you show a price?

Marketplace prices change with every campaign, like 9.9, 11.11 and 12.12. A price here would go out of date quickly. The where to buy page links you straight to today’s price.

What warranty does it have?

The listing we checked states a 5-year local manufacturer warranty2. Warranty is regional, so buy from the official store in your country and keep your order confirmation.

Using it

Can it work as a UPS for my desk?

Yes. Leave it plugged into the wall with your devices plugged into it. If the power drops, it switches to battery in under 10 ms1. See the setup guide.

Is it safe to use indoors?

Yes. It has no engine and gives off no fumes, and it’s quiet, under 30 dB at light loads1. Keep it ventilated and out of direct sun.

Can it boil a kettle?

Heating appliances up to 1,200 W can run in X-Boost mode1. X-Boost lowers the voltage, so the kettle heats more slowly. Run it on its own, and expect it to use a large share of the battery.

How many devices can I plug in at once?

There are 3 AC outlets, 1 USB-C, 2 USB-A and a car outlet1. The AC outlets share 600 W. The runtime calculator warns you if you go over.

Charging

How long does it take to charge?

About 60 minutes from a wall socket, around 3.3 hours from a car socket, and as little as 1.5 hours from 220 W of solar in good sun1.

Can I use it while it charges?

Yes. That’s how the backup mode works: power passes through to your devices while it charges.

Travel

Can I take it on a plane?

No. Airlines don’t allow lithium batteries over 160 Wh in passenger luggage4, and RIVER 3 Plus is 286 Wh. For flights, take a power bank under 100 Wh.

Will it work in another country?

It’s built for the outlets and voltage of the region it’s sold in. If you’re moving abroad, check the plug type and voltage of your model on the listing.

Care

How long will the battery last?

The LiFePO4 cells are rated for 3,000 cycles before dropping to 80% capacity1. That’s years of daily use.

How should I store it?

Somewhere dry, ideally at 20–30 °C1. Top it up every few months if you’re not using it, and check the app for your battery level.
