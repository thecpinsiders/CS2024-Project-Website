Buying guide

Best power station for camping in Malaysia

A note first: we made this site for River 3 Plus, so read this guide knowing that. The checklist works for any brand, and our comparison table puts rivals side by side.

1. Size it to one warm night

A typical family night at a lowland campsite uses about 200Wh: a tent fan for 8 hours (120Wh), string lights and a speaker (about 55Wh) and two phone top-ups (30Wh). That points to a station of 250–300Wh. Smaller ones run out before morning, and bigger ones are heavier than a one-night trip needs. Plug your own devices into the runtime calculator to check.

2. Count the AC sockets

A fan, a projector and a laptop charger all want a socket. Many 300Wh stations have just one AC outlet, which means an adaptor and a guessing game. River 3 Plus has three, sharing 600W.

3. Pick LiFePO4 cells

Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries handle heat better and last thousands of cycles. Check the cycle count on the listing: 3,000 cycles is a weekend trip every week for more than ten years.

4. Check how fast it refills

If it's full from the wall in an hour, you can charge it while you pack the car. Car charging and a solar input matter for longer trips.

5. Buy local, and check the label

Buy from the brand or an authorised Malaysian reseller so the local warranty is valid, and look for the ST-SIRIM label when it arrives.

Home backup

How to keep Wi-Fi on during a blackout

When the power trips, the lights are the obvious loss. For anyone working from home, the bigger one is the internet: video calls drop, and the house goes quiet in the wrong way.

Power both boxes

Home fibre usually needs two boxes: the fibre modem (ONT) from your provider and the Wi-Fi router. Plug both into the power station. Together they typically draw 15–25W, so River 3 Plus keeps them running for roughly 10–16 hours.

Set it up as a UPS

Plug River 3 Plus into the wall, and the router and fibre box into River. It passes mains power through while it's there, and switches to battery in under 10 milliseconds when it isn't, fast enough that the connection doesn't drop.

Check the other end

If the power cut is wide enough to take out the street cabinet, your fibre goes down anyway. Keep your phone's mobile hotspot as plan B, and keep the phone charged from River's USB-C port.

Add a lamp and a laptop

An LED desk lamp (8W) and one laptop charge (70Wh) fit comfortably alongside. See a power-cut plan.

River 3 Plus vs a petrol generator

For decades, power away from the wall meant a petrol generator. For small loads, a battery station does the same job without the downsides.

Fumes

A generator's exhaust contains carbon monoxide, which is why it must never run in a tent, a home or a closed porch. A power station burns nothing, so it's safe beside you indoors and next to food at a stall.

Noise

River 3 Plus runs under 30 dB, about as loud as a whisper. A generator next to a campsite or a night-market stall is the loudest thing there.

Running costs

A full recharge of River 3 Plus uses about a third of a kilowatt-hour from the wall, a few sen at typical TNB home rates. A generator needs fuel every time it runs, plus servicing.

When a generator still wins

For big loads over many days, such as air-conditioning, a large fridge or power tools all day, you need a generator or a much larger station. River 3 Plus is for lights, fans, laptops, phones, routers and the like. See the full specs.

Home backup

Can a power station run a CPAP machine?

If someone at home sleeps with a CPAP, a night-time power cut is more than an inconvenience. Here's what River 3 Plus can and can't do. It isn't medical advice: talk to your doctor or equipment provider before you rely on any backup.

The short answer

Yes for most CPAP machines, with care. With the humidifier off, a CPAP usually draws 10–25W, so one charge lasts about 10–24 hours. With the heated humidifier on, it draws about 53W (ResMed lists this for the AirSense 10), so expect about 4.5 hours.

Turn the humidifier off during an outage

The humidifier's heater uses most of the power. Switching it off for the night can turn a few hours of backup into a whole night.

Oxygen concentrators are different

A home oxygen concentrator draws 250–350W, so River 3 Plus would run it for under an hour. It isn't a backup plan for one. Ask your provider about a backup cylinder or a much larger battery system.

Test it before you need it

Check the watts on your machine's power adapter, then run it from River 3 Plus for one normal night while the power is on. Keep River plugged in and charged, especially before the monsoon season. See the medical equipment table.

Camping

Your first family camp in Hulu Langat

Most first-time campers worry about the wrong thing. Setting up the tent takes twenty minutes. What actually ends the night early is the heat, the mosquitoes and a phone at 4% when the kids want one more photo.

Book the right site

Hulu Langat is under an hour from KL, which is why its riverside campsites fill up fast on weekends and school holidays. When you book, ask three things: is there a power point at the pitch, are there toilets and showers, and what happens if it rains hard.

Respect the river

The river is the reason you came, and the thing to watch. Heavy rain upstream can raise the water within minutes, even under a clear sky. Pitch well above the bank, keep the kids' swimming to daylight, and pack up straight away if the operator tells you to.

Plan for a warm, wet night

Lowland nights stay warm, and afternoon thunderstorms are normal, especially in April–May and October–November. Bring a groundsheet and a rain fly, and plan your power for the two things that matter after dark: light and air. A small USB fan running all night is the single biggest upgrade to a camping trip in Malaysia.

What we'd pack

  • Tent with mesh panels, a rain fly and a groundsheet
  • Mosquito repellent, and long, light sleeves for the evening
  • A USB fan and warm LED string lights
  • River 3 Plus, charged to 100% the night before
  • Water, more than you think, plus a rubbish bag to leave nothing behind

Work anywhere

Golden hour, flat batteries

Every location photographer knows the moment. The light is finally perfect, the couple is finally relaxed, and the camera flashes a red battery icon. The last spare went flat an hour ago.

Know your numbers

A typical mirrorless camera battery holds around 16Wh. River 3 Plus holds 286Wh, so even after conversion losses it can refill a camera battery more than a dozen times, or keep two chargers running all afternoon while you shoot.

Charge in rotation, not at the end

Set up the charger as soon as you arrive. Swap a battery in every time you change setups, and you'll finish the day with more full batteries than you started with.

Light and laptop from the same box

A 40W LED light for fill and a laptop on the 100W USB-C port can run together, well inside the 600W limit. Edit the first previews on site, and the client leaves with a smile instead of a promise.

Check the venue

Popular spots like Putrajaya's bridges, the Sekinchan paddy fields and the tea estates in Cameron Highlands can get busy at golden hour, and some locations need permission for commercial shoots. Sort that out first, then plan your power.

Care

Looking after a LiFePO4 battery in the tropics

Lithium iron phosphate cells are tough. River 3 Plus is rated for 3,000 cycles to 80% capacity. A few habits help you get all of them.

Keep it out of a parked car

River charges between 0 and 45 °C and works best at 20–30 °C. On a sunny afternoon, the inside of a car parked at a Malaysian R&R or a beach car park can get much hotter than that. Bring it with you, or leave it in the shade.

Give it air

Don't cover the vents with towels or sleeping bags, especially when it's running a fan or charging.

Get ready before the monsoon

Before the rainy season, charge it to full and keep it somewhere dry and off the floor, not in a garage that could flood.

Don't forget it in the cupboard

If it's sitting unused, top it up every few months so it doesn't drain too low. If it's set up as home backup, it stays plugged in and you never have to think about it.
